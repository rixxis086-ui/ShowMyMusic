# 🎵 ShowMyMusic v1.0.0 — Initial Release

Welcome to the initial release of **ShowMyMusic**, the ultra-modern, aesthetic music overlay for Windows 10 and Windows 11 inspired by Apple's Dynamic Island and macOS Liquid Glass materials.

---

### ✨ What's New in v1.0.0:

- 🎨 **18-Cluster Adaptive Color Engine**: Analyzes album artwork in real time and extracts vibrant, aesthetic dominant colors with smart contrast & luminance enhancement.
- 🌟 **GPU Hardware-Accelerated Neon Glow**: Gorgeous customizable halo glow with `RenderingBias="Quality"` for maximum visual fidelity.
- 🪟 **6 Curated Preset Themes**:
  - `🍏 iOS Liquid Glass`: Frosted glass with specular highlight reflections.
  - `⬛ Dynamic Island OLED`: Deep pure black floating pill aesthetic.
  - `🪟 Windows 11 Acrylic`: Fluent acrylic gradients and delicate border edges.
  - `⚡ Cyberpunk Neon`: High-voltage contrast with neon outline.
  - `❄️ Apple Light Frosted`: Bright frosted glass for light desktop themes.
  - `🎯 Minimalist Matte`: Clean, distraction-free solid matte surface.
- ⏱️ **Smooth 100ms Live Timeline**: Real-time GSMTC timeline interpolation without jumping or resetting.
- 🎛️ **Dual-State Visibility Modes**:
  - *Passive Mode*: Unobtrusive, click-through overlay while you work or game.
  - *Active Mode*: Reveals media controls (Play/Pause, Skip, Previous), volume badges, and timeline on mouse hover.
- 🖥️ **Multi-Monitor & DPI-Aware Positioning**: 8 standard screen zones plus drag-and-drop custom positioning across any monitor.
- ⌨️ **Global Shortcut & System Tray Integration**: Toggle visibility on demand via `Ctrl+Alt+M` or right-click tray menu.

---

### 📦 Download & Installation:

1. Download **`ShowMyMusic-win-x64.zip`** from Assets below.
2. Extract the archive to any folder of your choice.
3. Run **`ShowMyMusic.exe`**.
4. Play music in Spotify, Chrome, Apple Music, or Yandex Music — and enjoy!

*Requires Windows 10 (19041+) / Windows 11 and .NET 8.0 Desktop Runtime.*
